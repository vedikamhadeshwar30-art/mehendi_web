Preserving Sacred Traditions, Redefining Modern Elegance

Born and raised in the cultural heart of Rajasthan and trained under legendary master artisans in Jaipur and Mumbai, Riya has spent over a decade perfecting the delicate, symphonic geometry of Mehendi.

Her signature aesthetic marries the timeless grandeur of Marwari floral jaals, sacred hastamelap motifs, and royal peacock crests with the sleek fluidity of contemporary Indo-Western negative spacing and intricate mandala work.

Every bridal composition is treated as a one-of-a-kind artwork, customized with bride & groom portraits, the couple’s milestone dates, beloved pet outlines, or personalized wedding vows hidden subtly within the swirls.

The Secret to Deep Mahogany

4-Step Bridal Stain Care Ritual

Follow our studio-tested aftercare guide to achieve an ultra-dark, rich burgundy stain that lasts for weeks.

01

Natural Dry & Lemon-Sugar Seal

Allow mehendi to dry naturally for 45 minutes. Dab lightly with a warm lemon juice and cane sugar syrup to lock the paste firmly onto the skin.

02

Let Rest for 8-12 Hours

Keep the dried henna paste on overnight or for a minimum of 8 to 12 hours. Wrap hands in breathable cotton gauze to maintain natural body warmth.

03

Scrape with Mustard / Coconut Oil

Never wash with water! Gently scrape off dried crust using a blunt card and massage with warm mustard, coconut, or eucalyptus oil.

04

Clove Smoke & Avoid Water (24h)

Warm your hands over warm clove vapors (laung ki bhaap). Avoid water and soap contact for 24 hours to let the stain oxidize into deep mahogany.
