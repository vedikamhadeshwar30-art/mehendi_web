Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How early should I book my bridal mehendi?

We recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance for prime wedding season dates (October to March). For intimate ceremonies or party guest bookings, 2 to 4 weeks advance notice is advised.

Do you travel to home / wedding venue locations?

Yes! We provide home visits across Mumbai and destination wedding setups worldwide. You can select "Client Venue / Home Visit" during online booking.

When is the best time to apply bridal mehendi before the wedding?

Natural henna reaches peak rich burgundy stain 48 hours after application. We recommend applying mehendi 2 days prior to your primary wedding ceremony or reception.
